Privacy Policy for CoinLens

Effective Date: November 2026
Developer: Aaqil Pulle (Australia)
Contact: aaqil.pulle@gmail.com

CoinLens ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and financial data in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up using Email/Password or Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication, we collect your email address and unique user identifier (UID). Guest accounts use an anonymous Firebase UID.
  • Financial & Household Data: Information you voluntarily enter into the app, including income, expenses, budgets, property valuations, mortgage balances, superannuation, share holdings, and custom simulator scenarios.
  • Partner Sharing Data: If you invite a partner or link a household, your household dataset is shared in real time between the linked accounts.
  • AI Assistant Inputs (CoinBot): Text prompts, uploaded files, and images/screenshots you choose to send to CoinBot for analysis.

2. Device Permissions

  • Camera & Photo Gallery: Used strictly when you choose to capture or upload a receipt, bill, or financial screenshot for CoinBot to analyze.
  • Microphone: Used strictly when you tap the microphone button to dictate voice prompts to CoinBot via Android Speech Recognition.
  • Biometrics (Fingerprint / Face Unlock): Handled locally by Android's secure hardware BiometricPrompt API. CoinLens never accesses, stores, or transmits your biometric data.

3. How We Use & Store Your Data

  • Cloud Sync & Local Storage: Your financial entries are stored locally on your device and synced securely to Google Cloud Firestore.
  • AI Processing: Prompts and attachments sent to CoinBot are processed via Google's Gemini API solely to generate financial summaries or updates requested by you.
  • No Selling of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information to third parties or advertisers.

4. Data Security

All data transmitted between the CoinLens app, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and AI services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and encrypted at rest on Google Cloud servers.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You remain in full control of your data at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated Firestore records at any time from Settings > Delete Account inside the CoinLens app.
  • Email Deletion Request: You may also request permanent deletion by emailing aaqil.pulle@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 7 days.

6. Third-Party Services

CoinLens relies on Google Firebase (Auth & Firestore), Google Gemini API, and public market data feeds for live share prices.
